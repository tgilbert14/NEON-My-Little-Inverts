What am I looking at?

Every non-metabarcoding field opportunity is retained with one primary processing status. Separate support flags retain overlapping conditions, including sampling-impractical, unavailable-stratum, nonstandard, and reported-zero records. A usable zero without benthic area therefore remains area-unavailable for density while its reported-zero support flag is still counted. Missing taxon rows remain unknown, not a verified zero or absence. Quantitative views keep the exact event, water type, habitat, and sampler stratum; density is a collection record, not population size. EPT composition is descriptive, not a water-quality rating, and network comparisons are limited to effort and record counts. Patterns are descriptive, not causal.
