# Inverts Living Poster image provenance

## Workflow

The canonical source artwork was generated on 2026-08-03 with the built-in
ImageGen workflow using the prompt below. The reviewed source was saved as
`docs/assets/inverts-living-poster-v2.png`. Web delivery copies and the social
card were then produced locally by `scripts/build_poster_assets.py`; that script
does not add or derive scientific measurements.

## Final ImageGen prompt

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: responsive landing-page Living Poster hero artwork
Primary request: Create a static editorial screenprint of the hidden benthic micro-world in NEON aquatic invertebrate collection samples.
Scene/backdrop: Submerged cobbles and leaf litter on a stream-, river-, or lake-bottom; suggest field collection with one subtle generic enamel sorting tray edge and a small blank specimen tag, without showing any one sampler as universal.
Subject: A visually diverse group of freshwater macroinvertebrates recorded in collections: one mayfly nymph, one caddisfly larva in a small pebble-and-twig case, one freshwater snail, and one amphipod among the cobbles. Anatomically plausible enough to read as distinct organisms, but rendered editorially rather than as a scientific plate.
Style/medium: Bold mid-century editorial screenprint / linocut, layered ink, coarse halftone, paper grain, slightly imperfect registration, sophisticated museum-poster quality. Static artwork, not cartoon UI and not photorealistic.
Composition/framing: Wide landscape 16:9. Dominant organism micro-world on the right two-thirds. Preserve generous quiet dark negative space on the left third for HTML headline copy. Keep at least the mayfly, snail, and amphipod inside a central-safe crop so they survive mobile cropping.
Lighting/mood: Mysterious, curious, intimate underwater discovery; no purity glow, no heroic halo, no before/after story.
Color palette: Deep riffle teal and indigo-black, silt amber, warm cream, restrained oxidized coral accents. High contrast silhouettes with tactile ink texture.
Materials/textures: Rough paper, screenprint dots, carved-line texture in cobbles and leaf litter, subtle suspended sediment specks.
Constraints: No text, letters, numbers, labels, logos, watermark, borders, badges, checkmarks, score gauges, medical imagery, pristine-versus-polluted split, or health/clean-water symbolism. Do not imply absence, population abundance, water quality, ecosystem health, impairment, or causality. No decorative animation cues. Keep the left negative space genuinely uncluttered and dark.
```

## Editorial boundary

The Living Poster is editorial illustration. It is not a field photograph,
specimen voucher, sampling record, taxonomic plate, or data visualization. Its
organisms and setting invite exploration; they do not encode verified absence,
population abundance, water quality, ecosystem health, impairment, or cause.
